Dogear for Mac

Dogear turns books into audiobooks and talking books you can navigate by chapter,
heading, and page. Everything it needs is in the app, including its voice, so it works
without an internet connection. It needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and
macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

To install, drag Dogear into the Applications folder.

The first time you open it, macOS stops it. Dogear isn't signed by Apple yet: signing
needs a paid Apple Developer account, which the project hasn't got. To open it anyway:

On macOS 15 Sequoia and newer:
1. Open Dogear from Applications. macOS says it can't be opened. Choose Done.
2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
3. Near the bottom, next to the message about Dogear, choose Open Anyway.
4. Confirm with your password. Dogear opens, and from then on opens normally.

On macOS 14 Sonoma: in Applications, Control-click Dogear, choose Open, then choose
Open in the dialog. With VoiceOver: select Dogear in Finder, press VO-Shift-M to open
its shortcut menu, choose Open, then choose Open in the dialog.

If macOS still refuses, open Terminal and run this command, then open Dogear again:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Dogear.app

When Dogear opens, it opens a Terminal window that shows a link for your web browser.
Keep that window open while you use Dogear; close it, or press Ctrl+C in it, to stop
Dogear.

From Terminal, the dogear command works too:
/Applications/Dogear.app/Contents/Resources/dogear/dogear --help

To remove Dogear, drag it from Applications to the Trash.

These steps follow Apple's instructions for apps from unidentified developers. They
haven't yet been tried with Dogear on a Mac; if something differs, please tell us at
https://github.com/18hrshift/dogear-app/issues
